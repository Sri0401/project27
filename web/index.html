How the score works

Each area is ranked against all others on three indicators, then the percentile ranks are combined:

  • 30% public transport stops per km²
  • 25% share of land that is parkland
  • 30% rent as a share of household income (lower is better)
  • 15% injury road crashes per 1,000 residents, 2019–2023 average (lower is better)

A score of 80 means the area beats roughly 80% of areas on that weighted mix. It is a demonstration index, not an official measure.
